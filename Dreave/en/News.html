Dreave 1.0 Released — All features free to celebrate launch

→

Today, May 28, 2026, Dreave 1.0 is officially available on the App Store. We've been looking forward to putting Dreave in your hands, and that day is finally here.

To celebrate the launch, we're running a launch campaign — during the campaign period, everyone can use all features completely free.

  • Night Mode (REM cycle estimation × 40Hz acoustic stimulation)
  • Dream Journal (voice input, emotion tags, search)
  • Reality Check (random notifications for reality awareness)
  • Analytics (Recall Score, Growth Index)

Whether you're completely new to lucid dreaming or have tried before and given up — tonight is a great time to start. Give Dreave a place on your nightstand.
