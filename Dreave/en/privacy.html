Privacy Policy

Privacy
Policy.

Dreave respects your privacy and explains what data is collected, why it is used, when it is sent to third-party services, how long it is retained, and how you can request deletion.

Effective 2026.05.31 Last updated 2026.05.31

Overview

Dreave is designed to be local-first. Dream journals, sleep settings, and in-app records are generally stored on your device. However, certain features, including AI analysis, speech transcription, crash diagnostics, analytics, advertising, website analytics, and purchase management, may send necessary data to the third-party services described below.

Information We Collect or Use

  • On-device data: dream journal text, tags, emotion and lucid-dream records, sleep settings, notification settings, and app preferences.
  • Voice data: used for dream journal recording and transcription. We do not store voice files on our own servers.
  • AI analysis data: when you run AI analysis, the selected dream journal text is sent to an external AI service. If you include names, email addresses, or other identifying information in the text, that information may also be included.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: app launches, screen views, feature usage, crash logs, device type, OS version, app version, and anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers.
  • Advertising data: advertising identifiers, device information, ad impressions, clicks, reward events, and related interactions when ads are displayed.
  • Purchase data: App Store purchase and subscription status, receipt information, and purchase history. We do not collect credit card numbers or other payment card details.
  • Website analytics data: page views, referrers, browser and device information collected through Google Analytics on this website.

Purposes of Use

  • Providing dream journal, search, analysis, notification, Night Mode, and Reality Check features
  • Generating AI-based dream analysis, comments, and trend summaries
  • Transcribing voice input
  • Improving stability, diagnosing crashes, fixing bugs, and improving features
  • Displaying ads, measuring ad performance, and granting rewards
  • Verifying purchases, managing subscriptions, and responding to refunds and support requests
  • Preventing abuse, enforcing the Terms of Service, and complying with legal obligations

The App and website use the following third-party services. These providers are primarily located in the United States. Sharing Personal Information

We do not share personal information with third parties except as described in this policy, as required for App Store payment processing, with your consent, as required by law, or where necessary to protect life, body, or property.

Retention and Deletion

  • On-device dream journals and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Text sent for AI analysis is processed as necessary to generate analysis results. We do not permanently store AI analysis text on our own servers. Retention by third-party services is governed by their own policies.
  • Firebase Crashlytics crash-related data is retained according to Google's standard retention period, typically 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins.
  • Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, and AdMob usage data, advertising data, and website analytics data are retained according to each service's retention settings and standard retention periods.
  • Purchase and subscription records may be retained for accounting, tax, refund, fraud prevention, and support purposes for the period required by law or legitimate operational needs.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can delete on-device data through the app's delete functions or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can manage notification, speech recognition, tracking, and advertising identifier permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your App Store account settings.
  • To request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, erasure, suspension of third-party provision, or withdrawal of consent for personal data we control, contact us at the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request. Where required by law, we will respond within a reasonable period.
