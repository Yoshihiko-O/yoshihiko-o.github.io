About REM Cycle

Why 4.5 hours

Sleep repeats in 90-minute cycles. The REM period of the 3rd cycle — about 4.5 hours after falling asleep — is the longest and most vivid dreaming phase.

Dreave automatically estimates this "golden window" and delivers 40Hz acoustic stimulation. Research suggests 40Hz stimulation is associated with gamma wave activity, potentially inducing brain states similar to waking consciousness.
